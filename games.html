Arcade

Games

Three puzzles a day, the same for everyone. A new set at midnight where you are.

Cabinet 1

Starword

Find the day’s five-letter space word in six tries. Green: right letter, right place. Amber: in the word, somewhere else. Type, or use the keys.

Cabinet 2

Constellation

Name the constellation from its figure, north up and east to the left, as it looks in the sky. Each wrong guess tells you how far away the answer is on the sky, and which way.

    Cabinet 3

    Light Time

    How long has the light been on its way? Tune the dial, which runs in powers of ten from a second to the age of the universe, and lock it in. Five tries; within a factor of two wins.
